02 / About me

From customer relationships to marketing strategy.

During my studies, I had the opportunity to work for three leading international companies, gaining different yet complementary experiences that shaped my approach to marketing: strongly focused on customers, objectives and creativity.

Beauty Advisor

KIKO Milano

I combined my passion for beauty with sales techniques, working towards objectives and KPIs while developing first-hand knowledge of target behaviours and needs. This experience showed me how essential direct customer observation is when building truly effective communication and offers.

Image Consultant & Shop Assistant · Germany

Desigual

This international experience strengthened my communication skills, customer relations and results orientation. Working in a multicultural setting also improved my language skills and developed my adaptability, independence and sensitivity towards different contexts and audiences.

Marketing Assistant · Seville, Spain

The Coca-Cola Company

During a work-study project, I helped develop a marketing campaign for the launch of Coca-Cola packaging made from recycled material. It was one of my first hands-on marketing experiences and the moment I discovered how engaging I found the creative process: from defining the concept to its visual translation and the creation of the mockup.

The common thread

Three apparently different experiences, connected by skills I bring to my work in marketing today: customer insight, communication, results orientation, relationship building, adaptability, international awareness and creative thinking.

My approach to marketing grows precisely from the intersection of customer understanding, creativity and strategy.
